# Naming

**Final decision (owner): the product is called Screenroom.**

- Product name, documentation, code identifiers and future package/app IDs use
  Screenroom.
- The repository root is `~/Desktop/projects/screenroom`.
- Historical documents that describe the name as undecided or as a "backup" label
  are superseded. They are retained as history and must not be acted on.
- The alternate candidates and collision notes previously collected here are
  recorded in `reference/SCREENROOM_CHATGPT_HANDOFF.md`; none of them was approved.

## What this does and does not decide

Decided: the word mark, repository slug and internal naming.

Not decided, and not implied by the name being chosen: trademark clearance, app
store identifiers, bundle IDs, installer identity, icon/wordmark artwork, and any
public registration or expense. Those move with the release gates in
[`FEASIBILITY_GATES.md`](FEASIBILITY_GATES.md) and
[`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) (M7), and require the owner's
explicit instruction.

The name describes a room for your own screen(s). It must never suggest that the
product hosts, sells or acquires a catalog of content.
